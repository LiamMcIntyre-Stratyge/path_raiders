<objective>
Create the thin `src/lib/api/` seam (FND-05, D-07) wrapping ONLY the tables/RPCs scenes touch
today — `account.ts` (profiles), `rooms.ts` (rooms), `wallet.ts` (wallet read + credit RPC) —
and replace every direct `supabase.from('profiles'|'rooms')` call in `AuthScene`, `GameScene`,
and `LobbyScene` with calls to these clients.

Purpose: Scenes must reach persistent data only through a typed chokepoint so no scene issues
a direct write to an authoritative table. This is behavior-preserving — same reads/writes,
same outcomes — just routed through the seam. The fuller services layer + sim extraction are
Phase 10 (D-08); do NOT pre-build clients for tables/RPCs that don't exist yet.
Output: three `src/lib/api/*.ts` modules; three scenes refactored to use them.
</objective>
